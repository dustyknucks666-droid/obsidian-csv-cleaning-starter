Why Conventional Deduplication Destroys Business Data

Conventional Deduplication

Tools like Excel "Remove Duplicates" make silent, irreversible assumptions:

  • Forces you to choose between leaving conflicts unflagged or permanently deleting rows.
  • Silently discards updated customer phone numbers or addresses without a change log.
  • Leaves you with zero audit trail and zero way to recover erased records.

Our Non-Destructive Protocol

A deterministic, non-destructive process designed to protect business records:

  • 100% of rows are retained in the master file; zero rows deleted without authorization.
  • All formatting adjustments are logged cell-by-cell in a reversible audit trail.
  • Duplicates and conflicting values are flagged in an exceptions sheet so you stay in control.
