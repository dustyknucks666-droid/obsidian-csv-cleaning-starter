What Event Horizon Checks

  • Structure: Delimiter detection, encoding issues, ragged rows, unclosed quotes, blank/duplicate headers, and empty rows.
  • Records: Exact duplicate rows and conflicting records sharing an ID or email but differing in values.
  • Values: Missing fields, placeholders (N/A, null), invalid dates, ambiguous date formats, and unparseable numbers.
  • Safety: Formula-like cells starting with =, +, -, or @ that trigger spreadsheet calculation risks.
  • Import Hazards: Dropped leading zeros on SKUs/postal codes, truncated long numbers, and cell line breaks.

Out of Scope for Free Scanner

  • Excel binary workbooks (.xlsx, .xls) — export your sheet as CSV first.
  • Files over 25 MB or 100,000 rows — contact us for enterprise consolidation.
  • Automatic record deletion or file modification — our engine never guesses or overwrites your data.
